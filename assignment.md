# AI Agent Interns: Assignment and Next Steps (received 2026-10-07)

**Assignment:** Quincy → Platform Automation & Reliability → K.D.

Each intern helps develop an AI agent for a specific role within Colaberry: understand the job, work closely with the employee manager, and help train the agent to do useful, measurable work.

## 1. Research agentic AI
Research the differences between:
- a chatbot that answers questions
- an automated workflow that follows predefined steps
- an AI agent that uses tools to perform tasks
- an agentic system that plans, acts, checks results and adjusts
- a multi-agent system where agents collaborate

Focus on what the system actually does, how independently it operates, and when a human must step in. Apply this to the assigned role: what would a basic chatbot do, and what would a capable agentic employee do differently?

## 2. Research the agent's "body parts"
- **Brain:** reasoning and planning
- **Memory:** context and knowledge
- **Eyes and ears:** inputs (messages, documents, screenshots, system events, voice)
- **Voice:** communication; asks clarifying questions, explains decisions, admits uncertainty
- **Hands:** tools and actions
- **Skeleton:** role, responsibilities, boundaries, workflows, definition of done
- **Nervous system:** coordination; triggers, progress tracking, follow-ups, handoffs
- **Immune system:** safeguards; permissions, approvals, testing, error handling
- **Self-checking:** verification; checks results, recognizes failure, provides evidence

A manager should be able to ask "Why did you open this ticket?", "What are you waiting on?" and "What must happen before you close it?" and get an answer grounded in actual work. Tool access stays admin-controlled. Agents cannot create or grant themselves tools.

## 3. Study the job
Cover daily and weekly responsibilities; systems, documents and information used; decisions and approvals; common problems and repetitive tasks; how success is measured; and what must stay a human responsibility. Learn both the existing Data Analytics (DA) student processes and the new AI program processes. Document what exists today separately from what needs to be developed.

## 4. Book the first manager meeting (30 minutes)
Questions to ask:
- What do you do, and how do you do it?
- Can you walk me through a real task from start to finish?
- Which responsibilities support DA students today?
- How should those responsibilities work for AI students?
- What takes the most time or causes the most frustration?
- What could the agent handle, and what needs your approval?
- What would make this agent valuable to you?

Ask for examples of checklists, reports, templates and workflows.

## 5. Build an ongoing working relationship
Hold a recurring meeting with the manager at least once a week.

## First deliverable
- A short research summary with source links
- A map of the agent's components
- A summary of the role and its DA → AI responsibilities
- Notes from the manager meeting
- The first three proposed agent tasks, each with required information, tools, approvals and proof of completion
- The recurring weekly meeting schedule
